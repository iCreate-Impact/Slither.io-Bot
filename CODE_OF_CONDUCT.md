# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Slither.io Bot to be a welcoming project for anyone who wants to improve
the planner, measure sessions, write tests, or improve documentation. We pledge
to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and measurements, not people.
- Assume good faith, but be precise about evidence.
- Do not overstate results. Describe runs as they happened, with the result
  state, duration and stats.
- Respect other people's time: include commands, server, result state, and logs
  when reporting a problem.
- Do not post credentials, private data, or unrelated personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Encouraging or facilitating cheating, account abuse, disruption of other
  players, or attacks on game infrastructure.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public
spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, **ichidera**, through GitHub.
Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers
may remove content, close discussions, or temporarily/permanently ban
participants whose behavior violates this code.
